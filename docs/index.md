# EdTech Policy Framework

## Comprehensive Framework for Educational Technology Policy Development

> A structured approach to designing, implementing, and governing educational technology policies for K-12 and higher education institutions.

---

## Overview

This framework provides educational institutions with a structured approach to developing technology policies that promote effective teaching and learning while ensuring compliance, equity, and security. It addresses the full policy lifecycle from needs assessment through evaluation and continuous improvement.

## Key Principles

- **Equity-Centered**: Ensure all students have access to technology resources regardless of socioeconomic background
- **Evidence-Based**: Ground policy decisions in research on effective educational technology use
- **Privacy-First**: Protect student and educator data through robust governance mechanisms
- **Stakeholder-Inclusive**: Engage educators, students, families, administrators, and community partners
- **Iterative**: Build in regular review cycles to adapt to evolving technology landscapes

## Policy Lifecycle

```
Needs Assessment → Stakeholder Engagement → Policy Drafting → Review & Approval
        ↑                                                          ↓
   Evaluation ← Implementation ← Communication ← Final Publication
```
