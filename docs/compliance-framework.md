# Compliance Framework for Educational Technology

## 1. Regulatory Landscape

Educational institutions must navigate a complex web of federal, state, and institutional regulations when deploying technology. This framework provides a systematic approach to ensuring compliance across all regulatory domains.

## 2. Federal Regulations

### FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act)
**Applicability:** All institutions receiving federal funding

**Key Requirements:**
- Protect the privacy of student education records
- Provide parents/eligible students access to their records
- Obtain written consent before disclosing personally identifiable information
- Maintain records of disclosures to third parties

**EdTech Compliance Actions:**
- Classify all data elements in technology systems as directory vs. non-directory information
- Execute FERPA-compliant data sharing agreements with all vendors
- Implement role-based access controls limiting data visibility
- Maintain audit logs of all data access and exports
- Publish annual FERPA notification to students and families

### COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
**Applicability:** Services collecting data from children under 13

**Key Requirements:**
- Obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information
- Provide clear privacy policies describing data practices
- Allow parents to review and delete their child's information
- Implement reasonable data security measures

**EdTech Compliance Actions:**
- Maintain a registry of all technology tools used with students under 13
- Obtain vendor certifications of COPPA compliance
- Implement school consent processes where permitted
- Conduct annual review of data collection practices

### CIPA (Children's Internet Protection Act)
**Applicability:** Schools and libraries receiving E-Rate or LSTA funding

**Key Requirements:**
- Implement internet safety policies
- Deploy technology protection measures (content filtering)
- Educate students about appropriate online behavior
- Address cyberbullying awareness and response

### ADA and Section 508
**Applicability:** All public educational institutions and those receiving federal funds

**Key Requirements:**
- Ensure digital content is accessible to individuals with disabilities
- Meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards for web content
- Provide reasonable accommodations for technology use
- Procure accessible technology products

**EdTech Compliance Actions:**
- Include accessibility requirements in all procurement RFPs
- Conduct accessibility audits of existing technology platforms
- Provide alternative formats for digital content upon request
- Train content creators on accessible design principles
- Maintain an accessibility complaint and resolution process

## 3. Compliance Audit Framework

### Annual Audit Cycle

| Quarter | Focus Area | Activities |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Q1 | Data Privacy | Review vendor agreements, audit data access logs, verify consent processes |
| Q2 | Accessibility | Test platforms against WCAG standards, review accommodation requests |
| Q3 | Security | Penetration testing, incident response drill, policy review |
| Q4 | Comprehensive | Annual compliance report, policy updates, training assessment |

### Audit Procedures

#### Data Privacy Audit
1. Inventory all technology systems processing student data
2. Verify current data sharing agreements are on file for each vendor
3. Review data access logs for unauthorized access patterns
4. Test data deletion and portability procedures
5. Verify consent mechanisms are functioning correctly
6. Document findings and remediation timelines

#### Accessibility Audit
1. Automated scanning of web properties using WAVE or axe tools
2. Manual testing with assistive technology (screen readers, keyboard navigation)
3. Review of procurement records for accessibility certifications
4. Assessment of accommodation request fulfillment rates
5. User testing with individuals with disabilities
6. Gap analysis and prioritized remediation plan

#### Security Audit
1. Vulnerability scanning of all public-facing systems
2. Review of access control configurations
3. Testing of incident response procedures
4. Assessment of encryption practices (data at rest and in transit)
5. Review of third-party security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001)
6. Phishing simulation and security awareness assessment

## 4. Vendor Compliance Management

### Vendor Assessment Checklist
- [ ] Signed data processing agreement with FERPA provisions
- [ ] COPPA compliance certification (if applicable)
- [ ] SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent security certification
- [ ] Accessibility conformance report (VPAT/ACR)
- [ ] Data breach notification procedures documented
- [ ] Data deletion/portability capabilities confirmed
- [ ] Subprocessor disclosure and approval process
- [ ] Insurance coverage verification

### Ongoing Vendor Monitoring
- Annual recertification of compliance documentation
- Review of vendor security incident disclosures
- Monitoring of vendor financial stability
- Assessment of vendor product accessibility updates
- Review of data processing activity logs

## 5. Incident Response

### Data Breach Response Protocol
1. **Identification** (0-1 hour): Detect and confirm the breach
2. **Containment** (1-4 hours): Isolate affected systems, preserve evidence
3. **Assessment** (4-24 hours): Determine scope, affected records, root cause
4. **Notification** (24-72 hours): Notify affected individuals, regulators, and law enforcement as required
5. **Remediation** (1-4 weeks): Address vulnerability, restore systems, update controls
6. **Review** (30 days post-incident): Post-incident analysis, policy updates, lessons learned

### Notification Requirements
| Regulation | Notification Timeline | Required Recipients |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| FERPA | Without unreasonable delay | Affected students/parents, Department of Education |
| State breach laws | Varies (typically 30-60 days) | Affected individuals, state attorney general |
| COPPA | Promptly | FTC, affected parents |

## 6. Training and Awareness

### Required Training Programs
| Audience | Training Topic | Frequency | Delivery Method |
|----------|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| All staff | Data privacy basics | Annual | Online module |
| IT staff | Security best practices | Quarterly | Workshop + online |
| Faculty | FERPA and student data | Annual | Department meeting |
| Administrators | Compliance overview | Annual | Executive briefing |
| New employees | Technology policies | Onboarding | Online module |

## 7. Documentation and Record Keeping

### Required Records
- Signed data sharing agreements (retain for duration of relationship + 3 years)
- Compliance audit reports (retain for 7 years)
- Training completion records (retain for 5 years)
- Incident response documentation (retain for 7 years)
- Consent forms and records (retain for duration of enrollment + 5 years)
- Policy version history (retain indefinitely)
