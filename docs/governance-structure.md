# Governance Structure for Educational Technology

## 1. Overview

Effective EdTech governance requires clear organizational structures, defined roles, and transparent decision-making processes. This document establishes a governance model that balances centralized oversight with distributed input from all stakeholders.

## 2. Governance Model

### Tiered Decision-Making Framework

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│          Board of Trustees/Regents       │  ← Strategic approval
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│      Executive Leadership Council        │  ← Budget and priority alignment
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│     EdTech Governance Committee          │  ← Policy development and oversight
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│        Domain Working Groups             │  ← Specialized recommendations
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│     Stakeholder Advisory Panels          │  ← Community input and feedback
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Decision Authority Matrix

| Decision Type | Authority Level | Approval Required |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Strategic technology direction | Executive Leadership | Board ratification |
| Policy creation/major revision | Governance Committee | Executive approval |
| Technology procurement (>$50K) | Governance Committee | Executive approval |
| Technology procurement (<$50K) | Domain Working Group | Governance Committee review |
| Operational procedures | IT Department | Governance Committee notification |
| Emergency technology decisions | CTO/CIO | Governance Committee ratification within 48 hours |

## 3. Committee Structures

### EdTech Governance Committee

**Purpose:** Central decision-making body for all educational technology matters.

**Composition:**
- Chief Technology Officer / Chief Information Officer (Chair)
- Chief Academic Officer / Provost
- Chief Financial Officer or designee
- Data Privacy Officer
- Accessibility Coordinator
- Faculty representatives (minimum 3, from different departments)
- Student representative
- Parent/family representative
- IT infrastructure lead
- Instructional design lead

**Responsibilities:**
- Approve technology policies and standards
- Review and approve major technology procurements
- Oversee technology strategic plan alignment
- Monitor compliance and risk
- Resolve cross-functional technology disputes
- Commission and receive working group reports

**Meeting Cadence:** Monthly, with emergency sessions as needed

**Quorum:** Simple majority with at least one representative from academic, technical, and stakeholder categories

### Domain Working Groups

Specialized groups that research, evaluate, and recommend within specific domains:

#### Learning Technology Working Group
- Evaluates instructional platforms and tools
- Reviews pedagogical effectiveness evidence
- Recommends adoption, continuation, or retirement of learning tools

#### Data and Privacy Working Group
- Monitors regulatory compliance
- Reviews vendor data agreements
- Investigates data incidents
- Recommends privacy-enhancing practices

#### Infrastructure and Security Working Group
- Assesses network and hardware needs
- Reviews cybersecurity posture
- Plans capacity and disaster recovery
- Recommends infrastructure investments

#### Digital Equity Working Group
- Monitors access gaps across student populations
- Evaluates equity impact of technology decisions
- Recommends interventions for underserved communities
- Reviews accessibility compliance

### Stakeholder Advisory Panels

**Purpose:** Provide ongoing community input to inform governance decisions.

- **Faculty Panel:** Monthly pulse surveys, semester forums
- **Student Panel:** Focus groups, user experience testing, feedback channels
- **Family Panel:** Annual survey, town hall meetings, communication preferences
- **Community Panel:** Quarterly meetings with employers, libraries, community organizations

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### Chief Technology Officer (CTO) / Chief Information Officer (CIO)
- Chairs the EdTech Governance Committee
- Accountable for technology strategy execution
- Reports to executive leadership on technology performance
- Ensures regulatory compliance across technology systems

### Data Privacy Officer
- Monitors compliance with FERPA, COPPA, and state privacy laws
- Reviews all vendor agreements for data provisions
- Manages data breach response procedures
- Conducts privacy impact assessments for new technologies

### Accessibility Coordinator
- Ensures all technology meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- Reviews procurement decisions for accessibility compliance
- Provides training on accessible content creation
- Manages accommodation requests related to technology

### EdTech Coordinators (Departmental)
- Serve as liaisons between departments and central IT
- Support faculty with technology integration
- Collect and report departmental technology needs
- Facilitate professional development at the department level

### Faculty Technology Champions
- Model effective technology use in instruction
- Mentor peers in technology adoption
- Participate in pilot programs for new technologies
- Provide feedback to working groups and governance committee

## 5. Decision-Making Processes

### Standard Policy Decision Process
1. Issue identified through stakeholder input, compliance review, or strategic planning
2. Working group assigned to research and develop recommendations
3. Working group presents findings and options to Governance Committee
4. Governance Committee deliberates and votes (simple majority)
5. If approved, forwarded to Executive Leadership for resource allocation
6. Board approval sought for major strategic or financial decisions
7. Implementation plan developed with timelines and accountability

### Technology Procurement Process
1. Need identified and validated against strategic plan
2. Requirements documented with input from end users
3. Market analysis and vendor evaluation using standard rubric
4. Pilot program (minimum 1 semester for major adoptions)
5. Pilot evaluation with stakeholder feedback
6. Governance Committee review and approval
7. Contract negotiation with legal and privacy review
8. Board approval if above financial threshold
9. Phased deployment with training and support plan

### Emergency Decision Protocol
- CTO/CIO may make unilateral decisions for security incidents or service disruptions
- Must notify Governance Committee within 24 hours
- Committee ratification or revision within 48 hours
- Post-incident review within 30 days

## 6. Accountability and Reporting

### Performance Metrics
| Metric | Target | Reporting Frequency |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Policy compliance rate | >95% | Quarterly |
| Technology uptime | >99.5% | Monthly |
| Support ticket resolution | <24 hours average | Monthly |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | >80% positive | Annual |
| Accessibility compliance | 100% for new procurements | Per procurement |
| Budget variance | <5% of allocated | Quarterly |

### Reporting Structure
- Working groups report to Governance Committee monthly
- Governance Committee reports to Executive Leadership quarterly
- Annual comprehensive technology report to Board
- Public-facing annual technology transparency report

## 7. Conflict Resolution

When stakeholders disagree on technology decisions:
1. Facilitated discussion within the relevant working group
2. If unresolved, escalation to Governance Committee with written positions
3. Governance Committee mediates and makes binding decision
4. Appeal to Executive Leadership for extraordinary circumstances only
5. All decisions documented with rationale for transparency
