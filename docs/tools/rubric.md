# Technology Evaluation Rubric — scorer

Score a tool against the [rubric](../technology-evaluation-rubric.md). Weights (25 / 20 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 10), the 0–4 scale and the three minimum thresholds — every Privacy and Security criterion at least 2, WCAG compliance at least 2, overall weighted score at least 2.5 — are the document's own. Leave a criterion unscored and the overall renormalises over what is scored and reports **Incomplete**. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

The result can be copied as Markdown for the recommendation report described in the rubric's evaluation process.
